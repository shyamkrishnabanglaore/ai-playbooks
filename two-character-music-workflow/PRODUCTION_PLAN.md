# Production Plan and Prompt Examples

## Runtime scaffold
The default is 190 seconds, 38 distinct five-second scenes. At a genuinely constant 96 BPM in 4/4, each shot spans two bars. Generated music may differ: measure the accepted track and revise the table before animation.

| Section | Time | Duration | Scenes | Function |

| Chorus 1 | 0:40–1:00 | 20s | S009–S012 | Perform four simple signature actions together. |

| Final chorus | 2:40–3:00 | 20s | S033–S036 | Celebrate through the signature actions with a visual payoff. |
| Outro | 3:00–3:10 | 10s | S037–S038 | Settle side by side and wave; resolve the music cleanly. |

## Music prompt
Create an original cheerful children's learning song about [TOPIC], featuring two friends [A] and [B]. Use simple Indian English, a memorable repeated hook, short rhythmic lines, clear vocals, playful acoustic percussion, ukulele, light piano and bells. Aim for approximately 190 seconds with a brief instrumental introduction, three developing verses, repeated choruses, a gentler bridge and a resolved instrumental outro. Suggested tempo 96 BPM, 4/4. Keep vocals clear and the arrangement cohesive. No borrowed melody or lyrics. The children in the video act and dance while the soundtrack vocalist sings.

This is a creative brief, not a duration guarantee. If the live song-idea field is limited, enter a shortened topic there and place musical detail only in appropriate supported fields. Never exceed a field cap or invent a separate instruction field.

## Release checks
- Exact approved A/B identity and wardrobe in all scene images and clips.
- Both visible in every story scene; no unintended extra characters.
- Both mouth locks checked in motion, not just prompt text.
- One continuous clean song, zero clip audio, no gaps or duplicate clips.
- Actual final runtime 180–200 seconds and endpoint agreement within one output frame where measurable.
- Saved project and completed MP4 verified separately from queue submission.

## Cost planning
Base production is one accepted song, two canonical references if not supplied, approximately 38 approved start images and 38 approved video clips. Artistly draft images and optional scale/turnaround sheets are additional. Repairs and discarded generations consume extra credits. Read actual per-generation charges and show the user a maximum spend; do not present the base counts as a guaranteed bill or promise unlimited fixes.
